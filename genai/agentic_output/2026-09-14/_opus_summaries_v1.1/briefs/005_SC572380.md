## Summary

- Briarhill Properties Limited (SC572380) is an active Scottish private limited company incorporated on 28 July 2017, trading under SIC 68209 (other letting and operating of own or leased real estate), with two officers, both of whom have served since the company's earliest records. `[profile; officers]`
- Filed accounts are up to date: the latest are made up to 31 March 2025 with the next due 31 December 2026 and nothing overdue, though as at 30 September 2026 the most recent accounting reference date is some 18 months old. `[profile; computed]`
- The register carries 22 outstanding charges, all in favour of third-party lenders including Charter Court Financial Services (trading in part as Precise Mortgages), Kensington Mortgage Company, The Mortgage Works (UK) PLC and Paragon Bank PLC, secured on individual residential properties in Edinburgh, South Queensferry, Dalgety Bay, Middlesbrough and Billingham. Under SEC-01 we cannot offer a first-charge facility; any new lending ranks second unless the existing charge over that property is redeemed at completion. `[SC5723800023; SC5723800022; SC5723800021; SC5723800020; SC5723800019; SC5723800018; SC5723800017; SC5723800016; SC5723800015; SC5723800014; SC5723800013; SC5723800012; SC5723800011; SC5723800010; SC5723800009; SC5723800007; SC5723800006; SC5723800005; SC5723800004; SC5723800003; SC5723800002; SC5723800001; SEC-01]`
- Several pairs of charges were created within 30 days of each other for the same lender — for example the two Kensington Mortgage Company securities of 19 September 2025 over 140 Carrick Knowe Drive and 51 Carrick Knowe Road, and the two Kensington Mortgages fixed charges of 9 February 2022 over 19 Southmead Avenue and 35 Kentmere Road, Middlesbrough. SEC-06 treats this as tranched facilities, so full redemption figures for all charges are needed before any refinance is quoted. `[SC5723800023; SC5723800022; SC5723800013; SC5723800012; SC5723800015; SC5723800014; SC5723800002; SC5723800001; SC5723800007; SC5723800006; SEC-06]`
- Eleven of the outstanding charges contain a negative pledge, including those held by Charter Court/Precise and The Mortgage Works over 59 Carrick Knowe Hill, 51 Carrick Knowe Road, 46 Carrick Knowe Hill, 25 Walker Drive, 11 Pentland Court, 19 Southmead Avenue, 35 Kentmere Road, 51 Moorcock Close and 51 Low Grange Avenue. Under SEC-07 no further charge may be taken over those properties without the existing lender's written consent, evidenced before any offer. `[SC5723800020; SC5723800019; SC5723800018; SC5723800017; SC5723800016; SC5723800015; SC5723800014; SC5723800011; SC5723800010; SC5723800007; SC5723800006; SEC-07]`
- One charge to Aldermore Bank PLC created on 30 November 2020 is recorded as fully satisfied; under SEC-03 it is disregarded for ranking and is simply evidence of earlier borrowing capacity. `[SC5723800008; SEC-03]`
- Accounts have been filed late in each of the last three years — 30 December 2023, 31 December 2024 and 30 December 2025, by two to three days each time — which CON-03 treats as a pattern of late filing rather than an isolated lapse, and is a key reason for the referral. `[AA filed 2025-12-30; AA filed 2024-12-31; AA filed 2023-12-30; CON-03]`
- The latest accounts are total-exemption full accounts, which do not disclose turnover or profit; this is missing information rather than an adverse signal, and under CON-04 we simply need management accounts covering the most recent 12 months before any facility is offered. `[profile; AA filed 2025-12-30; CON-04]`
