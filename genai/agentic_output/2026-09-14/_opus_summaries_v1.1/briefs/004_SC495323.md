## Summary

- H&H (EDI) LTD (SC495323) is an active private limited company incorporated on 16 January 2015, operating in real estate activities (SIC 68100, 68201, 68209, 68320). It has a single active officer, who is the only officer ever appointed. `[profile; officers]`
- The company has four outstanding charges, all in favour of The Royal Bank of Scotland PLC: one created 25 May 2017, one 7 September 2017 and two on 15 January 2019. The 2019 charge SC4953230003 is secured on the property at 10 Gordon Terrace, Edinburgh, and SC4953230002 on 20 Holyrood Road, Edinburgh. `[SC4953230001; SC4953230002; SC4953230003; SC4953230004]`
- Because these charges are outstanding to a third-party lender, no first-charge facility can be offered — any new lending ranks second unless the existing charges are redeemed at completion. The two charges created on the same day in favour of the same lender point to a structured or tranched facility, so full redemption figures for all charges are needed before any refinance is quoted. `[SEC-01; SEC-06; SC4953230003; SC4953230004]`
- Three of the charges (SC4953230001, SC4953230003 and SC4953230004) contain a negative pledge, so RBS's written consent must be evidenced before any further charge is created over the same security. Charge SC4953230001 is also a floating charge over the whole undertaking, which materially limits what remains available as security — refer for assessment of unencumbered assets. `[SEC-07; SEC-08; SC4953230001; SC4953230003; SC4953230004]`
- Filing conduct is good overall: nine of the ten accounts filings were made on or before the due date, the exceptions being the 2020 accounts filed 92 days late in January 2021 and the 2019 accounts filed 3 days late. The latest accounts (to 31 January 2025) were filed 41 days early and nothing is currently overdue, with the next accounts due 31 October 2026. `[AA filed 2016-10-01; AA filed 2017-10-27; AA filed 2018-10-16; AA filed 2019-10-31; AA filed 2021-01-28; AA filed 2021-10-25; AA filed 2022-10-10; AA filed 2023-09-01; AA filed 2024-09-25; AA filed 2025-09-17; profile]`
- The main non-security issue is currency of information: the latest accounts are made up to 31 January 2025, some 19.9 months before the assessment date, so the financial position on record is no longer current. `[CON-06; computed; profile]`
- As the accounts are total-exemption full, they disclose no turnover or profit; this is missing information rather than an adverse indicator. Obtain management accounts covering the most recent 12 months before any facility is offered. `[CON-04; AA filed 2025-09-17]`
