## Summary

- McCulloch Enterprises 2 Limited (SC618946) is an active Scottish private limited company incorporated on 23 January 2019, operating in buying and selling of own real estate (SIC 68100), with three active officers and no new appointments in the last 12 months. `[profile; officers; computed]`
- The company has two outstanding charges, both in favour of third-party lenders: one created 8 July 2026 to The Mortgage Works (UK) PLC, secured on 45 Kings Road, Rosyth, Fife, and one created 16 July 2024 to Lendinvest BTL Limited, secured on 38 Lyneburn Crescent, Halbeath, Dunfermline KY11 8DZ (title number FFE73301). `[SC6189460002; SC6189460001]`
- The decisive point is SEC-05: the charge to The Mortgage Works (UK) PLC was registered on 8 July 2026, within six months of the 30 September 2026 assessment date, so the company is presumed to have already satisfied its current funding requirement and should be deprioritised for outbound contact. `[SEC-05; SC6189460002; computed]`
- Even setting that aside, the existing third-party charges would prevent a first-charge facility (SEC-01), and the Lendinvest charge carries a negative pledge requiring that lender's written consent before any further charge over the same security (SEC-07). `[SEC-01; SEC-07; SC6189460001; SC6189460002]`
- Filing conduct is mixed rather than clean: accounts were filed 2 days late on 30 October 2025 and 3 days late on 31 October 2023, which counts as a pattern of late filing under CON-03, though an earlier 2021 filing was 87 days late. `[CON-03; AA filed 2025-10-30; AA filed 2023-10-31; AA filed 2021-01-23]`
- Financial visibility is poor: the company files micro-entity accounts that disclose neither turnover nor profit (CON-04), and the latest accounts are made up to 31 January 2025, some 19.9 months before assessment, so the position on record is stale (CON-06). Accounts are not currently overdue, with the next set due 31 October 2026. `[CON-04; CON-06; profile; computed]`
- Recommendation is to decline and hold off contact for now; if the relationship is revisited later, management accounts for the most recent 12 months and written consent from Lendinvest BTL Limited would both be needed before any offer. `[SEC-05; CON-04; SEC-07]`
