## Summary

- Briarhill Properties Limited (SC572380) is an active Scottish private limited company incorporated on 28 July 2017, operating under SIC 68209 (other letting and operating of own or leased real estate) with two officers, both in place since incorporation. `[profile; officers]`
- The company carries 22 outstanding charges, all in favour of third-party lenders including Charter Court Financial Services Limited (trading as Precise Mortgages), The Mortgage Works (UK) PLC, Kensington Mortgage Company Limited/Kensington Mortgages and Paragon Bank PLC, each secured on individual residential properties in Edinburgh, South Queensferry, Dalgety Bay, Middlesbrough and Billingham. Under SEC-01 no first-charge facility can be offered; any new lending ranks second unless the relevant charge is redeemed at completion. `[SC5723800023; SC5723800022; SC5723800021; SC5723800020; SC5723800019; SC5723800018; SC5723800017; SC5723800016; SC5723800015; SC5723800014; SC5723800013; SC5723800012; SC5723800011; SC5723800010; SC5723800009; SC5723800007; SC5723800006; SC5723800005; SC5723800004; SC5723800003; SC5723800002; SC5723800001; SEC-01]`
- Eleven of the outstanding charges contain a negative pledge, including those to The Mortgage Works (UK) PLC (2023-2024) and Charter Court Financial Services Limited over the Edinburgh, Dalgety Bay, South Queensferry and Teesside properties. Per SEC-07, written consent from each of those lenders must be evidenced before any facility is offered. `[SC5723800020; SC5723800019; SC5723800018; SC5723800017; SC5723800016; SC5723800015; SC5723800014; SC5723800011; SC5723800010; SC5723800007; SC5723800006; SEC-07]`
- Several lenders hold pairs of charges created on the same day - Charter Court (29 Jan 2019, 26 Apr 2022 and Nov 2019), Kensington Mortgages (9 Feb 2022) and Kensington Mortgage Company (19 Sep 2025) - which SEC-06 treats as structured or tranched facilities; full redemption figures for all charges are required before any refinance is quoted. `[SC5723800001; SC5723800002; SC5723800014; SC5723800015; SC5723800006; SC5723800007; SC5723800012; SC5723800013; SC5723800022; SC5723800023; SEC-06]`
- One charge to Aldermore Bank PLC created on 30 November 2020 is fully satisfied; under SEC-03 it is disregarded for ranking and is not itself a concern, though it evidences prior borrowing capacity. `[SC5723800008; SEC-03]`
- Accounts are not currently overdue (made up to 31 March 2025, next due 31 December 2026), but the last three accounts filings were each filed late by two to three days, which CON-03 treats as a pattern of late filing rather than an isolated lapse. `[profile; AA filed 2025-12-30; AA filed 2024-12-31; AA filed 2023-12-30; CON-03]`
- The most recent accounts are total exemption full and therefore do not disclose turnover or profit; under CON-04 management accounts covering the last 12 months must be obtained before a decision, and note that the accounts made-up date is now 18 months old as at 30 September 2026. `[profile; AA filed 2025-12-30; computed; CON-04]`
