- ESHAN LTD (company number 13104118) is an active private limited company incorporated on 30 December 2020, operating in other letting and operating of own or leased real estate (SIC 68209), with a single active officer who is the only officer ever appointed. `[profile; officers]`

- All four charges contain a negative pledge, so written consent from the existing lender must be evidenced before any further charge is created over the same security. `[SEC-07; 131041180004; 131041180003; 131041180002; 131041180001]`
- Filing conduct is poor: accounts have been filed late in each of the last three years — 94 days late on 30 September 2024, 67 days late on 3 September 2025 and 93 days late on 29 September 2026 — which is a pattern rather than an isolated lapse. `[CON-01; CON-03; AA filed 2024-09-30; AA filed 2025-09-03; AA filed 2026-09-29]`
