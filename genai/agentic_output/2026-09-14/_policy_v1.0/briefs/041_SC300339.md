## Summary

- Lochside Holidays Limited (SC300339) is an active Scottish private limited company incorporated on 5 April 2006, trading under SIC 55201 (holiday and other short-stay accommodation), with three officers, all of whom have been in place for more than 12 months. `[profile; officers]`
- The company has one outstanding charge, registered 5 February 2026 in favour of HSBC UK Bank PLC, which is a third-party lender outside the group. `[SC3003390001]`
- Because that third-party charge remains outstanding, no first-charge facility can be offered; any new lending ranks as a second charge unless the HSBC charge is redeemed at completion. `[SC3003390001; SEC-01]`
- The charge contains both a floating charge and a negative pledge, so written consent from HSBC must be evidenced before any further security is created, and an assessment of what unencumbered assets remain is required. `[SC3003390001; SEC-07; SEC-08]`
- Filing conduct is clean: every accounts filing since 2007 has been submitted ahead of its due date, and the company is not overdue, with the next accounts due 30 September 2027. `[profile; AA filed 2026-05-29; AA filed 2025-05-31; AA filed 2024-05-23; AA filed 2007-06-28]`
- The company shortened its accounting reference date in December 2025 to 31 December and filed micro-entity accounts to 31 December 2025 on 29 May 2026, following a long run of dormant filings up to 2025. `[AA01 filed 2025-12-05; AA filed 2026-05-29; AA filed 2025-05-31; computed]`
- Micro-entity accounts disclose no turnover or profit, so serviceability cannot be assessed from the filed figures; management accounts covering the most recent 12 months must be obtained before any decision. `[AA filed 2026-05-29; CON-04]`
