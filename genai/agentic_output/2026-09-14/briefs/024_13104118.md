- The company has two outstanding charges, both created on 9 February 2022 in favour of Charter Court Financial Services Limited, each secured on leasehold land at flat 2 Kavan Court, 51 Ancaster Road, Beckenham BR3 4DZ. `[131041180004; 131041180003]`
- Two earlier charges created on 4 February 2022 in favour of Lloyds Bank PLC are recorded as fully satisfied and are disregarded for ranking purposes, though they evidence prior borrowing capacity. `[131041180002; 131041180001; SEC-03]`
- Because third-party charges remain outstanding, no first-charge facility can be offered: any new lending would rank as a second charge unless the Charter Court charges are redeemed at completion. `[SEC-01; 131041180004; 131041180003]`
- All four charges contain a negative pledge, and the two Charter Court charges were created on the same day to the same lender, so written consent from the existing lender and full redemption figures for both charges must be evidenced before any facility or refinance quote. `[SEC-07; SEC-06; 131041180004; 131041180003; 131041180002; 131041180001]`
- Filing conduct is the main concern: accounts have been filed late three years running — 94 days late on 30 September 2024, 67 days late on 3 September 2025 and 93 days late on 29 September 2026 — which is a pattern rather than an isolated lapse and points to weak administrative control. `[CON-01; CON-03; AA filed 2024-09-30; AA filed 2025-09-03; AA filed 2026-09-29]`
- Accounts are currently up to date (made up to 30 September 2025, next due 30 June 2027) but are total-exemption full accounts that disclose no turnover or profit, so management accounts covering the most recent 12 months must be obtained before an offer is made. `[profile; computed; CON-04; AA filed 2026-09-29]`
